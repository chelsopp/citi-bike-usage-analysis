# 1. T-test: Difference in ride duration between member types
casual_duration <- citibike_nj_analysis %>%
  filter(member_casual == "casual") %>%
  pull(ride_length_min)

member_duration <- citibike_nj_analysis %>%
  filter(member_casual == "member") %>%
  pull(ride_length_min)

ttest_result <- t.test(casual_duration, member_duration, var.equal = FALSE)

cat("\n1. Welch Two Sample t-test (Casual vs Member Ride Duration in NJ):\n")
## 
## 1. Welch Two Sample t-test (Casual vs Member Ride Duration in NJ):
cat(sprintf("  t = %.2f\n", ttest_result$statistic))
##   t = 88.63
cat(sprintf("  df = %.1f\n", ttest_result$parameter))
##   df = 310262.9
cat(sprintf("  p-value = %s\n", 
            ifelse(ttest_result$p.value < 0.001,
                   "< 0.001",
                   sprintf("%.4f", ttest_result$p.value))))
##   p-value = < 0.001
cat(sprintf("  Mean difference: %.2f minutes\n",
            mean(casual_duration) - mean(member_duration)))
##   Mean difference: 8.68 minutes
cat(sprintf("  95%% CI: [%.2f, %.2f]\n",
            ttest_result$conf.int[1], ttest_result$conf.int[2]))
##   95% CI: [8.49, 8.87]

SUMMARY OF FINDINGS & INTERPRETATION

============================================================================

DATA CHARACTERISTICS

———————————————————-

  • The dataset consists of 961,796 rides from Jan-Dec 2023
  • All data is from the NJ Citi Bike system (Hoboken/Jersey City area)
  • Coordinates show 100% of rides originate west of -74.00° (NJ locations)
  • Top station: Grove St PATH (44,993 rides)

RIDER TYPES

———————————————————-

  • Annual Members: 73.8% of riders (709,452 rides)
  • Casual Riders: 26.2% of riders (252,344 rides)
  • Annual Members = Citibike subscribers with annual membership
  • Casual Riders = single-ride or short-term pass users

MONTHLY & SEASONAL PATTERNS

———————————————————-

  • Highest ridership: Summer (32% of annual rides)
  • Lowest ridership: Winter (16.7% of annual rides)
  • Peak month: August (109,316 rides, 11.4% of annual total)
  • Ridership increases steadily from Jan to Aug, then declines

DAILY & HOURLY PATTERNS

———————————————————-

  • WEEKDAY PATTERNS:
    • Peak hours: 5-6 PM (evening commute)
    • Secondary peak: 8 AM (morning commute)
    • Busiest day: Wednesday (148,602 rides)

WEEKEND PATTERNS:

  • Peak hours: 12-3 PM (midday leisure)
  • Different usage pattern than weekdays
  • Saturday & Sunday have similar ridership

RIDE DURATION

———————————————————-

  • Overall average: 11.2 minutes
  • Annual Members: 8.9 minutes average
  • Casual Riders: 17.6 minutes average (nearly 2x longer!)
  • Casual riders take significantly longer trips

BIKE PREFERENCES

———————————————————-

  • Classic bikes: 86.6% of rides (strong preference)
  • Electric bikes: 13.2% of rides
  • Docked bikes: 0.2% of rides
  • Both member types prefer classic bikes

STATISTICAL ANALYSIS RESULTS

———————————————————-

  1. T-TEST (ride duration by member type):
    • p-value < 0.001 (highly significant)
    • Casual rides are 8.68 minutes longer on average
    • 95% Confidence Interval: [8.49, 8.87] minutes
  2. CHI-SQUARE TEST (member type × day type):
    • p-value < 0.001 (highly significant)
    • X-squared = 16,403.81, df = 1
    • Strong association: casual riders use more on weekends
  3. ANOVA (ride duration across seasons):
    • p-value < 0.001 (highly significant)
    • F = 1,189, df = 3
    • Ride durations vary significantly by season

KEY TAKEAWAYS

———————————————————-

  1. The NJ Citi Bike system serves primarily as a COMMUTER SERVICE (74% annual members, peak weekday commute hours)

  2. Casual and Annual members have DIFFERENT USAGE PATTERNS: * Annual members: Short weekday commutes (8.9 min avg) * Casual riders: Longer weekend trips (17.6 min avg)

  3. Weather strongly impacts ridership: * Summer has 2x more rides than winter * Ride durations also longer in warmer months

  4. Despite electric bike availability, CLASSIC BIKES DOMINATE (86.6%)

RECOMMENDATIONS

———————————————————-

  1. Target casual riders with weekend/leisure promotions
  2. Ensure sufficient classic bike availability (high demand)
  3. Consider weather-based pricing or promotions
  4. Focus maintenance/availability during weekday commute peaks
